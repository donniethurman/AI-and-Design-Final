Research Sources

1. The Evolution of Authorship in the Age of Generative AI

Journal of Design Studies, 2024. Discusses how the definition of "author" is expanding to include curatorial practices when working with large language and image models.

2. AI Slop and the Crisis of Cultural Homogenization

Digital Culture Review, 2025. Explores the risk of aesthetic conformity when millions of creators use the same base models with similar prompts, leading to a flattening of visual culture.

3. Deskilling or Reskilling? The Designer's Toolbelt in 2030

UX Collective, 2023. An analysis of historical technological shifts (like the introduction of desktop publishing) and how they compare to the current AI revolution in creative fields.

4. Curation as Creation

Creative AI Institute, 2025. Argues that as the cost of producing raw assets approaches zero, the value of a designer shifts almost entirely to taste, selection, and editorial vision.

Designing With Intelligence

A conversation about AI, authorship, and the future of design.

Designer A

If AI generates the final output, what happens to the craft? Design is about skill, not just prompt engineering.

Designer B

The tools change, but the intention remains. Automation handles the tedious tasks, freeing us to focus on the core concept.

Designer A

But what about originality? An AI just recombines existing work. It’s a synthesis of theft.

Designer B

Every designer builds on the past. AI assistance is simply a new mechanism for curation and reference. The originality is in the synthesis we direct.

Designer A

Now we're drowning in generative content. The ease of production leads to AI slop—a sea of mediocre, thoughtless images.

Designer B

True, we have a creative abundance, but that just shifts our role from solitary creator to discerning curator. We choose what matters.

Designer A

If we all use the same models, won't our work suffer from cultural homogenization? Everything starts looking the same.

Designer B

Only if we let the model drive. The designer's direction—our taste, our prompt, our edit—is the wedge against conformity.

Change is inevitable. The real question is how designers choose to harness it.
